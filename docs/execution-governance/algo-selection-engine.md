# Algo Selection Engine

Scores algos on capital, risk profile, market condition, paper-test fit, liquidity, volatility/drawdown/complexity penalties.

Beginner blocked: short_straddle, short_strangle, naked_options, heavy_leverage, hft_scalping, martingale, double_after_loss.

Allowed examples: TV alert→paper, VWAP, RSI MR, MA cross, Supertrend, ORB, limited long options, rebalancing.

No strategy may promise guaranteed profit.
